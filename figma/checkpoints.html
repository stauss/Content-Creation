The design process, finally visible.

Checkpoint lets designers capture meaningful moments in their work, turn those moments into proof, and eventually share or monetize how great design actually happens.

Most platforms show the finished thing. Checkpoint shows how the finished thing became inevitable.

The Problem

Designers lose their thinking.

Designers do some of their most valuable work in the messy middle: half-built layouts, rejected directions, client pivots, visual experiments, and subtle refinements that never make it into the final case study.

When it is time to build a portfolio, all of that context has to be recreated from memory. The result is either polished fiction or messy file history.

Checkpoint turns design process into a first-class artifact.

Phase 1

The Checkpoints Plugin

The first release is a Figma plugin and widget system that lets designers save versioned states of selected frames, groups, or nodes directly from the canvas.

Think of it as save points for design work. A designer selects a frame, creates a checkpoint, and the plugin stores that moment as a visual snapshot.

Create
Save a selected node with a shortcut or button.
Preview
See thumbnails, groups, counts, and current state.
Return
Navigate, compare, delete, move, or restore checkpoints.

Why it works

It is valuable immediately. A designer does not need a profile, audience, publishing workflow, or team to benefit. The plugin solves a personal pain first: remembering and navigating the evolution of work.

Gotchas

  • Snapshot storage must stay lightweight.
  • Reverting needs to be safe and predictable.
  • The UI must avoid becoming a heavy project-management system.
  • Designers need confidence in what was saved and what will happen when restoring.
Phase 2

Portfolio from process.

Once designers are capturing meaningful snapshots, those snapshots become the raw material for portfolio pieces.

Instead of starting with a blank page, a designer selects key checkpoints, adds light annotations, and generates a clean process story.

  • Select checkpoints to include in a public case study.
  • Reorder checkpoints into a narrative arc.
  • Add short notes, decisions, or captions.
  • Generate shareable project pages and lightweight profiles.
  • Hide messy, private, or confidential work.
Portfolios generated from real work, not reconstructed after the fact.
Phase 3

A destination for how design actually happens.

The long-term version of Checkpoint is a design process platform: not another gallery of final shots, but a place to publish curated work in progress, learn from designers, and monetize the knowledge behind the work.

Discover
Browse projects by process type, not just final category.
Follow
Subscribe to designers, releases, tutorials, and behind-the-scenes updates.
Monetize
Sell deep process access, annotated files, tutorials, templates, and walkthroughs.
